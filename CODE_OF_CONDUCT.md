# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive and technically rigorous community. Participants must treat others with respect regardless of background, identity, experience level, language or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Give actionable, evidence-based feedback.
- Assume good intent while addressing technical and safety risks directly.
- Respect privacy, vendor licenses and responsible vulnerability disclosure.
- Clearly distinguish verified hardware behavior from assumptions or simulations.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and help repair their impact.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of secrets, unsafe device instructions presented as verified facts, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues privately through the repository's security advisory/private reporting channel. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close contributions, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation or permanently ban participants according to severity and recurrence. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
